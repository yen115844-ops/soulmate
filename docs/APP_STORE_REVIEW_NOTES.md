## Notes for Reviewer (paste into "Notes" field)

**App name:** [Your app name]

**App concept - Guideline 1.1.4 compliance**

This app is a **social activity platform** where users discover and connect with others who share similar interests. Users can browse profiles, find people with matching hobbies, and send invitations to do activities together (e.g. coffee, movies, sports, travel, events).

**Key clarifications:**

- **No compensated dating or companionship.** Users do **not** pay each other for time, companionship, or services. There are no hourly rates, per-session fees, or any form of payment between users for meeting up.
- **Free activities.** All activities and meetups between users are **completely free**. No credits, tokens, or currency are exchanged between users for activities.
- **Not a dating app.** The app is focused on **shared interest activities** (sports, dining, travel, events), not romantic matching. There are no swipe-to-match or dating features.
- **Premium subscription (IAP).** Revenue comes exclusively from **auto-renewable subscriptions** purchased via In-App Purchase. Premium subscribers get enhanced features like priority visibility, more activity invitations per day, and profile badges. Premium does **not** enable any form of payment between users.

**Guideline 3.1 - In-App Purchase**

All monetization is through Apple In-App Purchase (auto-renewable subscriptions):
- `premium_1w` - Premium 1 Week
- `premium_1m` - Premium 1 Month
- `premium_3m` - Premium 3 Months
- `premium_12m` - Premium 12 Months

There are no other payment mechanisms in the app. No external payment links, no wallet, no user-to-user payments.

**Safety features (Guideline 1.1.4)**

- Profile verification via selfie/liveness check
- In-app reporting and blocking
- Emergency SOS button during activities
- Content moderation for messages and photos
- Community guidelines enforcement

**Similar apps on the App Store**

- **Meetup** - Find and join local activities with people who share your interests
- **Bumble BFF** - Find friends and activity partners
- **Eventbrite** - Discover and attend local events and activities

## Metadata checklist

- **App description:** Describe as a "social activity platform" or "interest-based activity community." Emphasize finding friends and activity partners, NOT dating or companionship services.
- **Keywords:** Use terms like "activities," "hobbies," "friends," "meetup," "social," "interests." Avoid "dating," "companion," "escort," "partner" (in paid context).
- **Screenshots / previews:** Show activity browsing, interest matching, invitation flow, and safety features. Do NOT show any pricing, hourly rates, payment screens, or wallet.
- **App category:** Consider "Social Networking" or "Lifestyle" category.
- **Privacy policy:** Ensure it reflects the social activity model, not service marketplace.
